About me

The engineer who speaks both kilovolts and code

I am an Electrical & Solar PV Engineer and Energy Analyst with 3+ years of hands-on experience designing, bidding, commissioning and operating solar PV, hybrid and grid-connected systems in Uganda, South Sudan and Sierra Leone. I've worked on everything from rooftop-scale off-grid kits and solar water pumps, to a 50 MW grid-connected PV portfolio feeding Sierra Leone's national grid through a 161 kV connection on the Bumbuna–Freetown corridor.

What makes me different is that I sit at the meeting point of field engineering and data. I know how real inverters, transformers and protection relays behave at 3 a.m. during a fault — and I use Python, MATLAB/Simulink and machine-learning models to turn that behaviour into insight: spotting underperformance early, quantifying losses and supporting smarter operations, including LLM-assisted O&M.

I'm a Gold Medalist in Electrical Engineering (CGPA 8.31/10), an IEEE-indexed author, a former Mitacs Globalink research intern in Canada, and I'm currently pursuing a Master's in Sustainable Energy and Development (SEDev) at Europa-Universität Flensburg, Germany.

pv_performance.py
import pandas as pd
from sklearn.ensemble import IsolationForest

# Compare actual vs expected yield and flag underperforming inverters
df = pd.read_csv("scada_daily.csv", parse_dates=["date"])
df["PR"] = df["energy_kwh"] / (df["irradiation_kwh_m2"] * df["kwp"])

model = IsolationForest(contamination=0.03).fit(df[["PR", "availability"]])
df["anomaly"] = model.predict(df[["PR", "availability"]])
print(df[df.anomaly == -1][["date", "inverter", "PR"]])  # ⚡ fix before kWh are lost
